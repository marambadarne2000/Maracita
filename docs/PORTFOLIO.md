# Portfolio walkthrough

## The problem

Appointment-led businesses often use disconnected calendars, payment notes, customer chats and spreadsheets. That makes cancellations, unpaid visits and customer follow-up easy to miss.

## The Maracita approach

Maracita turns daily operations into a clear flow:

1. Configure people, services and availability.
2. Build a customer profile and schedule the right service with the right team member.
3. Track arrival, payment and completion states.
4. Surface the next useful action through Smart Rescue.
5. Keep the full customer journey, receipt history and feedback in one place.

## Screens worth exploring

| Area | Purpose |

## Honest implementation notes

This is a working product prototype, not a claim that third-party payment or messaging providers are live. Live billing, automated communications, public deployment and compliance work belong to the production integration phase. Keeping that boundary explicit is intentional product and engineering practice.
